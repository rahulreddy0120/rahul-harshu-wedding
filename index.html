WHAT TO WEAR

Dress Code Guide

Each event has its own vibe — dress to match the moment.

✨ Sangeeth

Glamorous Night

Festive Indian wear or evening wear. Go bold — this is the party night that kicks off the celebrations!

SUGGESTED COLOURS

Midnight Navy
Champagne
Emerald
Deep Burgundy
Black
⚠️

Avoid: Casual or overly casual outfits

💡 Heels & dress shoes recommended — it's a glam night. Thursday Nov 12.

💍 Engagement

Festive & Bright

Semi-formal Indian wear. Sarees, lehengas, kurtas welcome. Bright festive colours encouraged.

SUGGESTED COLOURS

Royal Purple
Fuchsia
Coral
Teal
Gold
⚠️

Avoid: All-white or all-red (reserved for bride at wedding)

💡 Noon ceremony on Friday Nov 13 — comfortable yet festive.

🌿 Mehendi

Vibrant & Festive

Semi-formal Indian wear. Salwar kameez, lehenga, kurta. Western semi-formal also welcome.

SUGGESTED COLOURS

Parrot Green
Hot Pink
Sunny Yellow
Deep Orange
⚠️

Avoid: Heavy bridal colours (red, maroon)

💡 Friday night Nov 13 — comfortable flats recommended, lots of dancing!

☀️ Haldi

Casual & Colorful

Casual wear you don't mind getting turmeric on! Yellow is strongly encouraged.

SUGGESTED COLOURS

Sunshine Yellow
Mango Orange
Cream
White
⚠️

Avoid: Dark colours (they stain more visibly)

💡 Saturday noon Nov 14 — wear clothes you're okay ruining, turmeric is forever!

🪷 Wedding Ceremony

Formal & Elegant

Formal Indian or Western attire. Sarees, lehengas, sherwanis, suits welcome.

SUGGESTED COLOURS

Ivory
Champagne Gold
Blush Pink
Sage Green
Royal Blue
⚠️

Avoid: All-white or all-red (reserved for bride)

💡 Sunday noon Nov 15 — the couple's palette is ivory & gold, complementary colours are perfect.
